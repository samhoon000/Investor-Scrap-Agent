Brad Flora is a General Partner at YC. He was co-founder and CEO of Perfect Audience, an ad network funded by Y Combinator in 2011, acquired by Marin Software in 2014 and used by companies like Eventbrite, Atlassian, and New Relic to market to more than a billion people. He is an active angel investor, occasional contributor for Slate.com and lives in San Francisco with his wife and three children. He has a BA from Princeton University in English and an MS in Journalism from Northwestern.
Brad has done more than 3,500 office hours over the course of 9 batches.
The YC companies Brad has worked with are worth a combined $34 billion.
